FAQs

How is gratuity calculated?

Under the Payment of Gratuity Act: 15 × last drawn (basic + DA) × years of service ÷ 26.

Who is eligible for gratuity?

Usually employees with 5 or more years of continuous service. Rules can differ for fixed-term employees, so check with your employer.

How are months of service counted?

For employers covered by the Act, 6 months or more in the last year counts as a full year.
