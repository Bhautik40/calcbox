FAQs

Which cities count as metro for HRA from FY 2026-27?

Eight cities get the 50% limit: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. All other cities use 40% of basic.

Can I claim HRA in the new tax regime?

No. HRA exemption is only available under the old tax regime.

Do I need my landlord’s PAN?

Usually yes, if your yearly rent is more than ₹1 lakh. Keep rent receipts or a rent agreement as proof.
