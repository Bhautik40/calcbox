FAQs

How many square feet are in an acre?

One acre is 43,560 sq ft, which is about 4,047 square metres.

How many square feet are in a cent and a guntha?

One cent is about 435.6 sq ft. One guntha is about 1,089 sq ft (40 guntha = 1 acre).

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32. So 37 °C is 98.6 °F.
