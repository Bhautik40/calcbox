FAQs

How is home loan eligibility calculated?

Banks check how much EMI you can afford, usually up to 50% of net monthly income minus existing EMIs, then work out the loan that EMI can repay.

How much home loan can I get on ₹50,000 salary?

At 8.5% for 20 years with no other EMIs, about ₹28.8 lakh. Your credit score and the bank’s rules can change this.

How can I increase my eligibility?

Add a co-applicant with income, choose a longer tenure, close small loans and keep a credit score above 750.
