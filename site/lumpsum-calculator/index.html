FAQs

What is a lumpsum investment?

Investing a single amount at once, instead of monthly instalments like a SIP.

How is lumpsum return calculated?

Future value = amount × (1 + annual return)ⁿ, where n is the number of years.

Lumpsum or SIP, which is better?

Lumpsum can earn more if markets rise steadily. SIP spreads your risk over time and suits monthly income.
