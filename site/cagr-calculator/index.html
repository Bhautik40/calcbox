FAQs

What is CAGR?

Compound Annual Growth Rate is the steady yearly growth rate that takes an investment from its start value to its end value.

How is CAGR calculated?

CAGR = (End value ÷ Start value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1.

What is a good CAGR?

For Indian equity over the long term, 10–15% is considered good. For FDs, CAGR is simply the interest rate.
