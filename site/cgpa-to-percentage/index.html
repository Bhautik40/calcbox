FAQs

How do I convert CGPA to percentage for CBSE?

Multiply your CGPA by 9.5. A CGPA of 8.4 equals 79.8%.

Do all universities use × 9.5?

No. Many universities use their own formula, such as CGPA × 10 or (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. Check your marksheet or university rules.

What CGPA is first class?

Usually 60% and above is first class and 75% and above is distinction, but rules vary by university.
