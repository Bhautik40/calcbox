FAQs

How do I calculate a discount?

Discount = price × discount % ÷ 100. The final price is the original price minus the discount.

Is 20% + 10% the same as 30% off?

No. A 10% extra discount applies on the already reduced price, so 20% + 10% is 28% off in total.

How do I find the discount percentage?

Discount % = (original price − sale price) ÷ original price × 100.
