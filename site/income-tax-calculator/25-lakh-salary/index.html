Income Tax on ₹25 Lakh Salary

FY 2026-27 · New vs old regime

₹3,19,800 tax (new regime)

For a salaried person earning ₹25 Lakh a year in FY 2026-27, income tax is ₹3,19,800 under the new regime and ₹5,22,600 under the old regime (assuming ₹1.5 lakh of deductions like 80C). You save ₹2,02,800 with the new regime. In-hand pay is about ₹1,81,683 a month before PF and other deductions.
